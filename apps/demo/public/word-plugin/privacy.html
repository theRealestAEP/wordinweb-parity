Privacy, in plain English.

I’m Alex Pickett, the developer of Word in Chat. It works in ChatGPT, Codex, Claude, and other apps that connect to MCP servers. I use document data only to provide the editing actions you request. These information pages contain no analytics, cookies, or tracking scripts.

Your document and chat.

The editor opens the .docx file you choose and processes it in the editor panel. Host-managed files save through ChatGPT or Codex when the host permits writing. For uploaded attachments, Download DOCX exports an edited copy through ChatGPT or your browser. In other apps, you choose the .docx in the editor, and it stays in your browser, or an assistant uploads it for you.

When you ask chat to read or edit your document, tool results may include text, formatting, comments, and document references. Choosing Add to chat sends the highlighted text and its references into the conversation. Reading or searching can return other parts of the document too.

The hosted connection.

Cloudflare hosts the connection between chat and the editor. Commands and results pass through it and may contain document text. When you open a ChatGPT attachment, the hosted server downloads that selected DOCX using ChatGPT’s temporary file URL and passes its bytes to the editor. It does not retain the file after returning that response. The connection holds active session state in memory and removes it when the editor disconnects or after about 90 seconds without editor polling.

Two kinds of file copy wait on the server for a short time. When an assistant uploads a .docx for the editor, the server keeps it in Cloudflare storage until the editor reads it, then deletes it. It deletes an unread copy after 10 minutes. When you or an assistant export an edited copy through a link, the server keeps that copy in memory until it downloads once, for at most 90 seconds.

Usage counts.

The server counts how the service is used, with no document content. It counts users each day as a one-way hash of the user ID that ChatGPT sends, or of a network address and app version when the app sends no ID. It also counts which tools run, the types of failures, how long the editor takes to load, and file sizes in broad ranges, such as “under 10 MB”. These counts contain no file names, document text, or readable network addresses.

OpenAI processes your conversation and tool results under its policies and your account settings. Cloudflare processes hosting traffic under its applicable policies. Their records and retention are separate from the plugin’s temporary session state. See OpenAI’s privacy policy and Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Support and privacy requests.

Email sent to alex@cobbery.com reaches my support mailbox with your address, message, and attachments. I use that information to respond to your request. I keep support emails in my mailbox until I delete them. Remove private document details from anything you send.

You can contact me at the same address to ask about access, correction, or deletion of information held by me. Use the account controls of your chat app, such as ChatGPT, Codex, or Claude, to manage conversation history held by its provider.

Scope and updates.

This notice covers the Word in Chat and its information pages. Other WordInWeb services have their own data flows. Updates to this notice will appear here with a revised date.
